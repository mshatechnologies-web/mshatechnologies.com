01 / About

A small studio, working one product at a time.

No large team, no venture funding — just a builder shipping tools people actually reach for.

MSHA Technologies started with a simple observation: most people manage their money and spot fraud using scattered SMS alerts, half-read bank notices, and guesswork. We build software that reads that noise for you, and tells you plainly what's going on.

Every product we ship stays free at its core, works on modest devices, and is built to hold up under real Indian conditions — patchy networks, regional languages, and messages that don't come in tidy English.

Founder & Managing Director

Mr. MSHA — built by one person, one problem at a time.

MSHA Technologies is run by a single founder who writes the code, talks to the first users, and reads every support email personally. There's no large team behind the name — just someone who got tired of watching people get scammed over text and lose track of their own money, and decided to fix both.

The approach stays deliberately small: pick one real problem, build a focused tool for it, ship it, and only then move to the next. Bharat Budget App and CyberScamCheck are both products of that same discipline — built for how people in India actually use their phones, not how a textbook says software should look.
